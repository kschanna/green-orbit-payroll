Getting started

Sign in with your Green Orbit account. The main menu is grouped into People, Time & Billing, Payroll, and Automation. Select a heading to expand it. The current section opens automatically, and your other expanded sections are remembered.

On a phone or tablet, use the menu button to open the same navigation. Notifications and Help remain available as standalone items.

People

Organisations

Organisations hold employing-business and customer records. Create or edit an organisation with its name and 11-digit ABN. Configure an ABA profile before exporting bank payment files.

Workers

Workers contain identity, contact, tax, payment, superannuation, leave, document, and portal-access information. Open a worker to manage those records. Deactivate a worker when appropriate instead of deleting records needed for payroll history.

Assignments and work patterns

An assignment links a worker to a customer and defines billing arrangements. A work pattern records employment and pay terms, ordinary hours, frequency, rates, and effective dates. Check that effective dates cover the intended work and pay period.

Time & billing

Timesheets

Create daily or weekly timesheets against the correct assignment and pay component. Review dates, hours, and status before approval. Approved time can feed invoicing and payroll.

Timesheet Runs

Timesheet Runs generate and manage scheduled timesheets for a period. Filter by customer, worker, or status; review generated entries; then save and approve them when correct.

Invoices

Generate invoices only from eligible approved time. Confirm the customer, assignment, billing rates, source timesheets, totals, and status before progressing an invoice. PDF previews remain available from the invoice list.

Payroll

Payroll readiness

Run readiness checks before payroll. Resolve critical worker-setup and timesheet findings using the provided links; review warnings before deciding whether to continue.

Pay Runs and payslips

Create a Pay Run for the correct organisation and period, process it, review worker calculations, then approve and complete it in order. Draft and unapproved runs are not completed payroll. Payslips show the resulting earnings, deductions, super, and net pay.

PBT Schedules

PBT (Pay, Bill & Time) Schedules define customer cycles and generate scheduled periods. Check effective dates, frequency, generated-through date, and active status.

Pay Components

Pay Components classify time and payroll amounts such as ordinary hours or allowances. Configure definitions centrally and assign allowed components to assignments. Verify effective settings before use.

Documents, forms, and notifications

Open a worker’s Documents page to upload PDFs, preview them, and track processing status. Documents marked Ready for review require a human decision. Compare extracted values with the original, correct permitted values, and approve or reject the review.

Secure TFN and super forms submitted through the worker portal also require review before values are applied. Never request or copy a TFN through chat, ordinary messages, or Help enquiries.

Notifications report document, form, and compliance activity. Open a notification to follow its action link, or mark all as read after review.

Payments and ABA files

Prepare a payment batch from an eligible Pay Run, verify every recipient, bank destination, reference, and amount, then validate and download the ABA file. Manual direct-entry lines require a reason and are not payroll. Each export locks the batch and stores an immutable copy with its checksum. Open Payroll → ABA Files to review and download previous exports.

Financial reconciliation

Open Finance → Reconciliation to find issued invoices and exported payment batches ready to post. A posting snapshots the resolved account codes and names, cannot be generated twice for the same source, and is saved only when debits equal credits and the journal total agrees with the source transaction. Open any posting to inspect its journal lines, source trace, reconciliation result, checksum, or JSON export. Finance → Chart of Accounts shows the organisation-specific accounts used by posting rules.

Worker portal

Enable portal access from the worker record. Workers can view payslips, submit and review timesheets, check leave balances and request leave, see assignments, upload documents, complete secure payroll-setup forms, read notifications, and update permitted profile details.

Payroll setup items appear on the worker’s dashboard until complete or under review. Direct workers to the secure action shown there. Bank, tax, and super changes remain subject to verification.

Automation

Payroll Agent

The supervised Payroll Agent creates a draft scheduled timesheet, pauses for human review of hours, checks payroll requirements, and can prepare reconciled draft invoice and payroll records. Resolve linked blockers and use Retry when ready. It does not issue invoices, finalise payroll, or release payments.

AI Assistant

The AI Assistant can explain selected application data through approved read-only tools. Verify answers against linked source records. It cannot replace payroll approval or reveal sensitive tax and bank values.

Agent Monitor

Agent Monitor provides runtime diagnostics for automation tasks. Use it to investigate status and failures; use the underlying business records as authoritative.

Troubleshooting

  • If an assignment is unavailable, confirm the correct customer and effective dates.
  • If payroll or invoicing is blocked, check approval status, dates, rates, worker setup, and readiness findings.
  • If a document fails, confirm it is a readable PDF and upload a clean replacement.
  • If a worker cannot sign in, check portal access and account status.
  • Read the displayed error before retrying and contact your administrator if it persists.
