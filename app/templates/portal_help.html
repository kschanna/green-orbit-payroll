Quick answers

Where are my payslips?

Open Payslips to view and preview your pay history.

How do I submit a timesheet?

Open Timesheets, choose the assignment and period, enter your hours, then submit. Check its status for approval progress.

How do I request leave?

Open Leave to review balances and submit a request. It remains pending until an authorised person acts on it.

How do I complete payroll setup?

Use the actions on Home. Submit sensitive details only through a secure form or Documents.

How do I replace a rejected document?

Open Documents, delete the rejected copy, then upload the corrected PDF. Ask payroll for help if it cannot be deleted.

How do I change bank, tax, or super details?

Send a request here without sensitive values. Payroll will provide a secure path and verify the change.

What can the AI Assistant do?

Ask Green Orbit can explain your own timesheets, payslips, leave, assignments, documents, and notifications. It is read-only.
